Under the carpet

A soft floor is never just a floor

By the time a deck flexes underfoot, water has usually been in the wood a long time — and it rarely stopped at the floor. On the 1979 heirloom the floor, stringers and transom were all rotten through. On a 1996 Javelin the floor had broken clean through.

Rob’s answer in both cases was the same: open it up, replace the bad wood, glass it back in, then put the carpet and seats over a hull that’s actually sound.

  • He tells you first. One owner was told outright the boat would cost more to fix than it was worth — before any work started.
  • Quoted, then done. A price and a timeline up front, and a call when the opening comes around.
  • Family shop. One customer describes Rob and his son working the job together and explaining every step of it.

Marine upholstery

Sometimes the hull is fine and the inside is tired

Not every boat that comes to Rob is a rescue. Plenty just need what they sit on and walk on put right — carpet, seats, cushions, the pieces that decide whether a twenty-year-old boat feels twenty years old.

Carpet matched to what’s already there. Seats replaced, rebuilt, or swapped for a matched set that suits the boat. And the old eye-sore bench that’s bothered you for eight years finally gone.

  • Bass boats and center consoles — bass trackers, Javelins, Nitros and Makos all appear in his reviews by name.
  • Carpet-only jobs welcome. Removing and replacing carpet is, as one owner put it, a tough job he wouldn’t get to himself.
  • Matched, not approximate. One owner’s new carpet matched his existing carpet perfectly, and his replacement seats matched the boat.

Booking

Rob books ahead — and then he calls

One-man-plus-son shops that do this kind of work fill up, and Rob’s does. The part worth knowing isn’t that there’s a wait; it’s what happened when the wait was over. A bass tracker owner was quoted a price and a three-week build, told the next opening was six weeks out, and got a phone call six weeks later to start.

“His next opening was 6 weeks away. He called me 6 weeks later and started on my boat we selected premium heavy wieght marine carpet. Three weeks later it was done. The carpet made the boat look almost new in fact I have ordered new seats to complete the boat.” Robert W. · Google review

That was one customer’s job on one boat — not a promise about yours. Call Rob and ask what his next opening looks like and what your boat needs; he’ll tell you straight, including when it isn’t worth doing.
